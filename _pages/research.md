## Research Overview

My research interests lie in financial economics, with a particular focus on asset pricing, derivatives valuation, and financial risk management. I am interested in understanding how financial instruments are priced, how quantitative models capture financial risks, and how vulnerabilities within financial institutions and markets can develop into broader systemic risks.

My work combines theoretical and quantitative perspectives, with an interest in the application of analytical, numerical, and computational methods to financial economics.

## Research Areas

### Asset Pricing and Derivatives

I am interested in the valuation of financial instruments and derivatives, particularly option pricing and the application of quantitative models to complex financial products. My work explores analytical and numerical approaches, including Monte Carlo simulation and computational techniques.

### Financial Risk Management and Systemic Risk

This area focuses on the measurement and management of financial risks, the effectiveness of institutional risk-management frameworks, and the mechanisms through which weaknesses in governance, internal controls, and risk oversight can generate broader financial instability.

### Digital Assets and Stablecoins

I am interested in the financial economics of digital assets, particularly stablecoin structures, their pricing mechanisms, and the implications of different collateral and design arrangements for financial stability and systemic risk.

### Sustainable Finance and Geopolitical Risk

I am also interested in the relationship between sustainable finance and geopolitical uncertainty, particularly in how geopolitical risk can be incorporated into ESG frameworks and investment analysis.

## Research Methods

My research draws on quantitative and computational approaches, including:

- Derivatives pricing and financial modelling.
- Monte Carlo simulation and numerical methods.
- Partial differential equations and analytical techniques.
- Machine learning applications in financial economics.

The methodological approach depends on the specific research question and the financial problem being investigated.
